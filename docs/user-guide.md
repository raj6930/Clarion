# Clarion User Guide

## Getting Started

### Logging In
Navigate to your Clarion instance and sign in with your credentials. First-time users must be approved by an administrator before they can access the system.

### Dashboard
The dashboard is your home screen. It shows headline KPIs, case volume trends, SLA compliance, engineer workload, and cases requiring attention. You can switch between six layout presets (Editorial, Command Center, Executive, Operations, Magazine, Flow) from Settings → Preferences.

### Team View vs Account View
Toggle between Team View (cases owned by your team members) and Account View (cases for monitored customer accounts) using the buttons in the header bar.

## Cases
The Cases page shows all cases for your team or monitored accounts. Use filters to narrow by priority, status, owner, product, or date range. Click any case to see the full detail view with timeline, events, and AI-generated insights.

## Reviews
Quality reviews follow an AI-first workflow: the AI generates a complete draft review with per-question scores and coaching suggestions, and you refine it. To start a review, navigate to the case and click "Create Review", or accept an AI recommendation from the review queue.

### Scoring
Each question is scored 1-5 using the rubric definitions. Score 1 is Unacceptable, 3 is Acceptable, 5 is Excellent. When you change an AI score, provide a brief justification — the AI will re-evaluate its comments to align with your assessment.

### Finalising
Once you've reviewed all questions, add your overall comments and click Finalise. The review is locked and can be exported as PDF or emailed to the engineer.

## Analytics
The Analytics page provides deeper analysis: SLA compliance by priority, resolution time trends, engineer workload distribution, and AI-detected patterns. Use date range filters to compare periods.

## Predictions
Clarion predicts escalation risk for active cases using AI analysis of case attributes, communication patterns, and historical data. High-risk cases appear in the Attention Queue on your dashboard and in the Predictions page.

## Notifications
Alert rules monitor your cases and notify you when conditions are met (SLA breach, P1 no-update, escalation risk). You can configure alert rules, channels (in-app, email, Teams, Slack), quiet hours, and debounce intervals in Settings → Alert Rules.

## Chatbot
Click the Assistant icon to open the AI chatbot. It understands your current page context — if you're viewing a case, it knows which case. Ask questions like "Show me P1 cases", "What's our SLA compliance this week?", or "Create a review for case 00803992".

## Settings
Administrators can configure branding (colours, logo, company name), terminology (rename modules), AI engines, sync schedules, feature flags, and alert rules from the Settings page.
